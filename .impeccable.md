## Design Context

### Users

CaseJeeto serves two equal audiences in India. Clients, often under stress and without legal expertise, need to assess fit and credibility, understand the next step, and book trustworthy counsel quickly. Advocates need to present verifiable credentials, receive qualified discovery, and manage consultations and practice activity without feeling commoditized.

### Brand Personality

**Assured, Discerning, Human.** The experience should create calm confidence: premium in care and judgment, never exclusive, ceremonial, or intimidating. The product speaks plainly, treats evidence as reassurance, and respects both sides of the marketplace.

### Aesthetic Direction

**The Contemporary Legal Review.** A light-first editorial system inspired by a well-produced case digest or advocate brief: warm parchment fields, deep ink/navy structure, sandstone neutrals, rare seal-red emphasis, Literata display type, and Anek Latin interface text. Crisp rules, restrained corners, minimal shadows, dense useful product surfaces, and spacious marketing composition create a subtle document-and-court atmosphere.

Avoid purple SaaS gradients, gradient text, glassmorphism, oversized side-stripe callouts, bubbly components, generic equal-card grids, and decorative effects without informational purpose. Dark tokens exist for compatibility, but the launch experience is designed and verified light-first.

### Design Principles

1. **Evidence before decoration.** Credentials, courts, expertise, fees, availability, and next actions establish trust; ornament never competes with them.
2. **Editorial hierarchy, product clarity.** Use Literata to frame consequential ideas and Anek Latin for fast scanning, forms, tables, and operational detail.
3. **Premium through restraint.** Let proportion, typography, parchment tone, and 1px rules carry the character; reserve seal red for moments of decision or verified significance.
4. **Space by context.** Marketing pages breathe; search, profiles, and workspaces stay compact and information-rich. Avoid wrapping every section in a card.
5. **Calm is accessible.** Meet WCAG AA, preserve strong keyboard focus, provide 44px touch targets on coarse pointers, keep meaning independent of color, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
